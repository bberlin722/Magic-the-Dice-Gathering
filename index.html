How to play
  1. Roll. Everyone taps Roll and throws every die in their dice deck. All dice are visible to all players.
  2. Reroll. Secretly pick any dice to reroll once. Choices stay hidden until everyone locks in, then all rerolls are revealed together.
  3. Cast. Spend the mana you rolled to cast cards from your hand (max 5). Unspent mana is lost. Creatures cast this round are summoning: they can't attack, block or be targeted until next round.
  4. Attack. Split your attack between rival players and their active creatures. A player's active creatures absorb all damage aimed at that player, so kill them first. Each player's defense blocks incoming damage for them and their creatures. Then surviving creatures strike.
  5. Gold. Collect rolled gold plus 1, and a bounty for every creature you land the killing blow on.
  6. Initiative. Everyone rolls their initiative die to set the shopping order, highest first. Before you roll, you may pay gold to upgrade it: each upgrade adds +1 to every face (max +3). You can also buy upgrades in the shop.
  7. Buy. Everyone shops at the same time for new dice (up to 12), die face upgrades and initiative upgrades. Initiative order only decides who picks face-up market cards first: on your card pick, buy cards or pass the pick on. Three new cards join the shop every round; it holds at most six, and the oldest leave first.

Potions and healing are one use: they go to the exhaust pile (flame mark). Creatures that die are exhausted; creature cards swapped out of the shop or discarded go to the discard pile and get reshuffled. Everyone starts with 25 HP and a Guardian Totem. From round 20 the arena's fury burns every player for 1 more damage each round, so every duel ends. Last duelist standing wins.
